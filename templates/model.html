Step 1
Devanagari NLP Preprocessing

Marathi news headlines are processed to prepare the text for machine-learning classification. The preprocessing pipeline includes:

  • Unicode Normalization: Standardizes Devanagari Unicode representation for consistent text processing.
  • Noise Filtering: Removes URLs, HTML elements, unwanted symbols, and unnecessary punctuation.
  • Tokenization: Divides the cleaned Marathi headline into individual word tokens.
  • Stopword Removal: Removes commonly occurring Marathi grammatical words while preserving meaningful words and named entities.
Step 2
TF-IDF Vectorization

TF-IDF transforms the processed Marathi text into numerical feature vectors that can be used by machine-learning algorithms.

TF-IDF(t,d) = TF(t,d) × IDF(t)
  • N-Gram Range (1, 2): Uses individual Marathi words (unigrams) and consecutive word pairs (bigrams).
  • Numerical Representation: Converts textual information into weighted numerical features.
  • Model Input: The resulting TF-IDF vector is passed to the machine-learning classifier.
Step 3
Comparative Model Training

Three machine-learning classification algorithms are trained using the combined Marathi news corpus containing 12,056 cleaned samples across 10 target categories. A stratified 20% held-out test split is used for 10-class evaluation.

  • Multinomial Naive Bayes: A probabilistic classifier based on Bayes' theorem with Laplace smoothing.
  • Logistic Regression: A linear classification algorithm using learned decision boundaries and L2 regularization.
  • Linear SVM: A maximum-margin classifier designed for high-dimensional TF-IDF text features.
Step 4
Probability Calibration & Inference

The selected Linear SVM model is calibrated to produce interpretable class-probability estimates for the final news-category prediction.

  • CalibratedClassifierCV: Calibrates the Linear SVM using sigmoid calibration across 3-fold cross-validation.
  • Class Probabilities: Produces probability estimates for all 10 target news categories.
  • Selected Model: Linear SVM achieved the highest performance on the 10-class held-out test set.
  • 10-Class Test Accuracy: 84.78%
  • Weighted F1: 84.59%
