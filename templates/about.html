About The Project

Understanding Natural Language Processing, Indic language computation, and automated news categorization.

What is NLP?

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a specialized subfield of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and computational linguistics focused on enabling computers to understand, interpret, process, and derive meaningful insights from human languages in written and spoken forms.

In this project, NLP bridges human Marathi expression and statistical machine learning classifiers by transforming raw news text into structured mathematical vectors.

What is Marathi NLP?

Marathi is an Indo-Aryan language spoken by more than 83 million people, written in the Devanagari script. Marathi NLP presents unique linguistic challenges, including:

  • Agglutinative Morphology: Words often combine roots with postpositions (उदा. "शाळेमध्ये", "भारताचा").
  • Devanagari Unicode Encodings: Matras, viramas, and conjunct consonants require proper normalization.
  • Low Resource Setting: Fewer pre-labeled corpora compared to English.

What is Text Classification?

Text Classification is a supervised machine learning task where an algorithm learns to assign predefined category labels to unstructured textual documents based on their underlying linguistic content.

Given a Marathi headline \(d\), the system extracts high-dimensional TF-IDF features and computes the most probable category \(\hat{c} \in \{ \text{State, Entertainment, Sports} \}\).

Why is it Useful?

With thousands of regional articles published daily across digital portals and newspapers, manual categorization is slow and labor-intensive. Automated classification enables:

  • Automated News Portals: Instant routing of incoming Marathi articles into section pages.
  • Personalized Recommendation: Filtering feeds based on reader preferences.
  • Trend Monitoring: Tracking political, health, or economic issues in Maharashtra.

Project Objectives

1. Linguistic Preservation

Directly process Devanagari Unicode without lossy transliteration to English, preserving authentic Marathi orthography.

2. Multi-Model Comparison

Rigorously train and evaluate Multinomial Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, and Linear SVM on stratified test folds.

3. Production-Ready Deployment

Deliver a web application with responsive user interface and standardized REST API endpoints.
